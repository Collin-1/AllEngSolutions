Our Story

Built on experience, driven by purpose

Founded by Industry Professionals

All Eng Solutions (Pty) Ltd was founded by experienced professionals from the industrial supplies sector who saw an opportunity to better serve South Africa's public sector. Leveraging years of expertise gained through comprehensive training at the Bearingman Group, our team brings deep knowledge of industrial products, supply chain management, and customer service excellence.

We are committed to serving South Africa's government departments, municipalities, and state-owned enterprises with the same level of professionalism and product quality expected in the private sector — while adding the compliance credentials and transformation value that public procurement demands.

As a 100% Black-owned and proudly South African company, we are passionate about contributing to the nation's economic transformation goals while delivering real value through quality products and reliable service.
